Australia's Bushfire Crisis

Awareness & Action: Understanding the impact of wildfires on wildlife, climate, and communities. Australia has always faced bushfires, but recent years have seen unprecedented intensity. This project explores the causes behind these fires, the devastating consequences for biodiversity, and the resilience of the Australian people in the face of natural disasters. The Earth is what we all have in common. Protecting it starts with understanding the challenges it faces.
